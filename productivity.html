TIME IS MONEY

Morning 🌅

6:30 – 7:00 Wake Up & Move
Boost dopamine + alertness
7:00 – 8:30 Deep Skill Building I
Learn new concepts (hard stuff)
Strictly NO distractions
8:30 – 9:00 Break + Breakfast
9:00 – 10:30 Deep Skill Building II
Practice what you learned
Hands-on work (coding, building, etc.)
Replaces your "morning until 10" with a structured, scientifically optimal routine.

Midday 🧠

10:30 – 12:00 Project Work
Build something using what you learned
Focus: "How does it actually work?"
This is where real learning happens. Keep it up.
12:00 – 1:00 AI + Reflection
Test yourself first, then verify with AI
Ask questions & challenge answers
Fill knowledge gaps

Afternoon 🗣️

1:00 – 2:00 Rest & Recover
Critical for memory consolidation. Do not skip.
2:00 – 3:00 Communication Skills
Speak English (record yourself)
Learn vocabulary in context
Practice real conversations
3:30 – 4:30 Active Recall
Do something you like, but keep it useful to your goals.

The Golden Rule ⚡

"Don't just spend time — measure output."

Ask yourself at the end of every day:

  • What did I build today?
  • What concepts can I explain clearly?
  • What specific things did I struggle with?
